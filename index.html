A trust layer for a supply chain that badly needs one.

Honey

Overview

A digital trust and traceability system for honey provenance. Hive telemetry, accredited lab results and every custody transfer are written against a batch on-chain, so a consumer — or a regulator — can independently check what a jar actually contains.

Problem & Solution

The Problem

India's honey supply chain has a documented adulteration problem: syrup-cut product reaches shelves while genuine beekeepers lose price and reputation. Once honey is pooled, blended and rebottled, no record survives that anyone can verify.

The Solution

One tamper-evident custody record per batch. Sensors, lab verdicts and transfers all append to the same on-chain history, and a single QR scan resolves the chain back to the hive — returning verified, suspicious or recalled.

Workflow
01
HiveIoT sensors log acoustics, colony weight and micro-climate against a unique hive ID.
02
HarvestA batch is minted with origin, quantity and beekeeper identity.
03
LabSCIRA, HMF, moisture and pollen results are attached by accredited testers.
04
ChainRole-gated custody transfers are recorded by the Solidity contract.
05
ScanConsumer QR lookup returns verified / suspicious / recalled provenance.
Key Features
  • Smart beekeeping telemetry — per-hive acoustic, weight and micro-climate signals used to flag swarming risk.
  • Accredited lab verification — FSSAI / CSIR-CFTRI style testing including Stable Carbon Isotope Ratio Analysis to expose corn and cane syrup.
  • Role-based on-chain custody — a real Solidity contract with access control, so each actor writes only what they're authorised for.
  • Batch split, merge & mass balance — quantity validation that blocks duplicate reuse and phantom volume.
  • Consumer QR verification — provenance lookup with three explicit outcome states, not a vague trust badge.
  • Test coverage — 33 automated test cases across the contract and application logic.
